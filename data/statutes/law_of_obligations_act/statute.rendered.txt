Law of Obligations Act — selected provisions: § 53 (4), § 56 (1), § 56 (2¹), § 56 (2²)

§ 53. Application of provisions
(4) The right of withdrawal provided for in subsection 1 of § 56 of this Act shall not apply to contracts the object of which is:
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
1) the provision of a service or other continuous performance, where the contractual obligations of the trader arising from the contract are fully performed and the consumer pays a fee to the trader, only where the provision of the service or other performance has begun with the consumer's express prior consent and acknowledgement that the consumer is aware of losing their right to withdraw upon full performance of the contract by the trader;
[RT I, 24.11.2021, 1 – entry into force 28.05.2022]
1¹) the delivery of the goods, provision of services or other performance which price is dependent of fluctuations in the financial market which cannot be controlled by the trader and which may occur within the withdrawal period, including to contracts the object of which is the services related to foreign currency or the securities specified in § 2 of the Securities Market Act;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
2) delivery of goods manufactured taking into account the personal needs of the consumer who is a party to the contract;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
3) for delivery of goods manufactured according to the conditions established by the consumer;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
4) the delivery of such goods which deteriorate or expire rapidly;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
4¹) the delivery of such goods in sealed packaging which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons and were unsealed after delivery;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
4²) the delivery of such goods which are, after delivery, according to their nature, inseparably mixed with other items;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
4³) the delivery of such alcoholic beverages, the price of which has been agreed upon at the time of the entry into the contract of sale, the delivery of which takes place after more than 30 days and the actual value of which is dependent on fluctuations in the market which cannot be controlled by the trader;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
4⁴) the carrying out of urgent repairs or maintenance where the consumer has requested a visit from the trader for such purpose;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
5) the delivery of newspapers, magazines or periodicals with the exception of subscription contracts for an indefinite period for such publications;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
6) [Repealed – RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
7) the delivery of sealed audio or video recordings or computer software if the consumer has opened the package;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
7¹) the delivery for a fee of such digital content which is not delivered on a physical medium where the delivery thereof has begun with the consumer's prior express consent before the expiry of the withdrawal period and with the consumer’s acknowledgement that they are aware of the loss of their right of withdrawal, and the trader has given the confirmation specified in subsections 1 and 2 of § 55 of this Act;
[RT I, 24.11.2021, 1 – entry into force 28.05.2022]
7²) the provision of accommodation services, transportation of movables, use of motor vehicles, catering or services related to leisure activities if the trader undertakes to provide the services for a specific date or during a certain period upon conclusion of the above specified contract;
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
8) at auctions where the consumer is present or the consumer has been granted an opportunity to be present;
[RT I, 24.11.2021, 1 – entry into force 28.05.2022]
9) for travel and baggage insurance or other similar short-term insurance contracts entered into for a term of less than one month;
10) provision of financial services which have been performed by both parties to the contract at an express request of the consumer before the consumer exercises their right of withdrawal.
[RT I, 24.11.2021, 1 – entry into force 28.05.2022]

§ 56. Right of withdrawal from contract
(1) A consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving any reason.
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
(2¹) The deadline for withdrawal from a distance contract shall be deemed to have been observed by the consumer if the consumer has dispatched a notification concerning the withdrawal to the trader during the withdrawal period.
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
(2²) The consumer may withdraw from a contract by means of a standard form of application for withdrawal or by presenting another unequivocal application for withdrawal. The provisions of this section shall not apply to withdrawal from the contract for provision of a financial service.
[RT I, 31.12.2013, 1 – entry into force 13.06.2014]
